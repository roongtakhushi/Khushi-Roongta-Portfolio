# 🔧 Certificate Display Fix

## Issue Resolved
The certificates weren't displaying because PDF embeds don't work reliably in all browsers.

## Solution Implemented
Changed the certificate display to use **icon-based cards** instead of PDF embeds.

### What You'll See Now

**Certificate Card Front:**
- Large icon representing the certificate type (Python, trophy, shield, etc.)
- Certificate title
- Organization name
- Date earned
- "Click to view certificate" hint

**Certificate Card Back (on hover):**
- Full certificate details
- Organization and date
- "Click to View Full Certificate" message

**Click Functionality:**
- Click any certificate card to open the full PDF in a new browser tab

## How to Test

1. **Refresh your browser** (Ctrl+F5 or Cmd+Shift+R)
2. Scroll to the "Certifications & Achievements" section
3. You should now see **16 certificate cards** with icons
4. **Hover** over any card to see it flip and show details
5. **Click** any card to open the full PDF certificate
